|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|

| T-03-09-06 | Tampering | Open redirect | mitigate | Redirect only to homeFor(role); no request-supplied target |
| T-03-09-07 | Spoofing | Brute force on password login | accept | No rate limiter in free scope; synthetic data; recorded in HANDOFF |
