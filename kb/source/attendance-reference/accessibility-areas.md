# Accessibility areas

- **Visual:** adequate contrast, scalable text (A− | A | A+ control), clear typography, colour-independent status, zoom.
- **Hearing:** any audio cue needs an equivalent visual ("Attendance submitted successfully" with icon).
- **Motor:** keyboard navigation, larger controls, logical tab order, minimal steps, no precise/repetitive actions.
- **Cognitive:** simple language, consistent navigation, clear headings, step-by-step workflows, familiar icons, human error messages (not "ERR-ATD-503" but "Attendance could not be submitted. Please check your connection and try again.").
- **Language:** possibility of multi-language (English, Hindi, Marathi). Stretch for our project.

Formal academic framing: relate to **WCAG**. Our target: WCAG 2.2 AA.
