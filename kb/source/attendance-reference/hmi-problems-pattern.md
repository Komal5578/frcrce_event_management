# HMI problems to solve (original, attendance wording)

1. Record attendance with **minimum interaction time**
2. Prevent **accidental or duplicate entries**
3. Let faculty immediately recognise **absent/present/unverified**
4. Give clear **feedback after submission**
5. Let students understand status **without interpreting complex tables**
6. Highlight **shortage** without overwhelming the user
7. Make errors **detectable and easy to correct**
8. Support users with **different technical expertise**
9. Present information through **effective visualizations**
10. Maintain **consistency and visibility of system status**
