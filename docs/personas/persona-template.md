# Persona template

> **Assumed, pending research.** This persona is a hypothesis from the design team, not based on interviews. Replace it with evidence-based content once the team has run the research kit.

A persona is AI-drafted until the team replaces it with content from its own sessions. Keep the label line above in every copy of this template. Do not add an age, hometown, quote, statistic or percentage unless it comes from the team's real research and is cited. Scenarios, flows and requirements are not repeated here: cite `dom-scenarios`, `dom-flows` and `dom-requirements`.

## 1. Name, role and one-line description

_Source: `dom-personas` (Persona and Role columns)._

## 2. Primary goal

_Source: `dom-goals-tasks` (Primary goal) and `dom-personas` (Goal)._

## 3. Secondary goals

_Source: `dom-goals-tasks` (Secondary goals)._

## 4. Frequent tasks

_Source: `dom-goals-tasks` (Frequent tasks). Name the task that must take the fewest interactions._

## 5. Behaviours and tech comfort

_Source: `dom-personas` (Tech comfort). Mark each hypothesis "(assumed)"._

## 6. Pain points (hypotheses)

_Source: `dom-personas` (Pain point). Mark each "(assumed)". These are tested, not asserted._

## 7. Accessibility and context notes

_Consider the diverse-user groups in `src-evaluation-methods` section B (for example low vision, keyboard-only use, low technical confidence, small phone screens). Write only what is a hypothesis for this role._

## 8. HMI problems this persona drives

_List P ids from the numbering in `dom-hmi-problems`, with a one-clause reason each._

## 9. Research to confirm

_For each pain hypothesis, name the P ids that test it and the matching rows of `docs/research/question-trace.md`._

## 10. Status

Assumed, pending research. This becomes "Evidence-based" only after the team's sessions are run and the results are recorded; the note is then superseded by `dom-personas-evidence`.
