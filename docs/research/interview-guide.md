# Interview guide

> AI-drafted instrument. This file contains no responses, quotes or findings. Only the team may record results, and only real sessions may be marked evidence.

## How to use

Run one section per interview, matching the participant's role. Read the intro script aloud, ask the warm-up and core questions in order, use the probes only if the answer is short, then try the optional task observation. Every question ends with a tag such as [P3]; the tags link the question to one of the ten HMI problems. The key for P1 to P10 and the coverage matrix are in `question-trace.md`. Record notes on the blank sheet at the end, using a session id (S-01 style), never a name. Organizers are students who sit on an event committee; they are interviewed with Section B and answer its organizer questions.

## Section A: Coordinators

### Intro script (read aloud)

"Thank you for your time. We are students studying how college events are planned and managed, and we are designing a better system. This is voluntary. There are no right or wrong answers; we want to hear how things really work for you today. You can skip any question or stop at any time. We would like to take notes, and we will only record audio if you say yes. We will not record your face and we will not write your name; your notes are filed under a session id only."

### Warm-up

- Q-C01. Tell me about your role and the kinds of events you have helped plan at college. [P8]

  - Probes: How long have you done this? Who else helps you?

- Q-C02. Over the last year, which tools did you use to plan events (paper, chat groups, spreadsheets, notice boards, anything else)? [P8, P10]

  - Probes: Which one did you use most? Which did you avoid?

### Core questions

- Q-C03. Walk me through the last event you planned, from the first idea to sending it for approval. [P1, P6]
  - Probes: What took longest? Where did you wait for someone? What did you have to type or write more than once?

- Q-C04. The last time you chose a venue and time, how did you check that it was free? [P2]
  - Probes: Who or what did you ask? How sure were you afterwards?

- Q-C05. Over the last term, how often did a venue or time slot clash or get double-booked, and how was it found? [P2, P6]
  - Probes: Who found out, and when? What happened to the event?

- Q-C06. The last time you asked for approval, how did you find out what the answer was, and what did you understand had happened? [P4, P3]
  - Probes: What words did you see or hear? Was anything unclear about what to do next?

- Q-C07. Right now, how do you tell which of your events are still being prepared, which are waiting for a decision and which are confirmed? [P3]
  - Probes: Show me where you look. How long does it take?

- Q-C08. Tell me about the last time something was wrong in an event plan (a wrong date, venue or detail). How did you notice, and how did you fix it? [P7]
  - Probes: Could you change it easily? Did anyone have to be told again?

- Q-C09. How do you find out that few students have registered, or that a student's task is late? [P6]
  - Probes: Do you check, or does someone tell you? Is there too much to look at, or too little?

- Q-C10. Which lists, summaries or charts do you look at to judge how an event is going? What do they tell you, and what do they fail to tell you? [P9]
  - Probes: Which one do you trust most? Is there one you ignore?

- Q-C11. Which words do you and your colleagues use for an event that has been agreed (approved, confirmed, cleared, something else)? [P10]
  - Probes: Do students and the head of department use the same words? Has a different word ever caused confusion?

- Q-C12. Tell me about a time you or a colleague found a computer, phone or website hard to use for event work. [P8, P7]
  - Probes: What made it hard (comfort with technology, eyesight, small screen, bright light, slow internet)? What did you do instead?

### Task observation (optional)

- Q-C13. Please show me how you create an event and send it for approval today, saying aloud what you are doing. [P1]
  - Probes: Note the steps, the waiting points and any mistake the participant corrects. Do not help unless asked.

### Wrap-up

- Q-C14. Is there anything about planning events that we did not ask about but should have? [P8]

Thank the participant, explain what happens with the notes, and say that results will be shared if they wish.

## Section B: Students (including Organizers)

### Intro script (read aloud)

"Thank you for helping. We are students studying how college events work and we are designing a better system. This is voluntary. There are no right or wrong answers; we want to hear what really happens for you. You can skip any question or stop at any time. We would like to take notes, and we will only record audio if you say yes. We will not record your face and we will not write your name; your notes are filed under a session id only."

### Warm-up

- Q-S01. Tell me about the college events you took part in or helped organise this year. [P8]

  - Probes: How often? Did you ever help on a committee as an organizer?

- Q-S02. What phone or device do you mostly use for college things, and how do you usually get online? [P8]

  - Probes: Is the connection ever slow or missing? Do you use it outdoors?

### Core questions

- Q-S03. The last time you looked for an event to join, where did you look, and how did you tell whether it was still open? [P3]
  - Probes: Where did you hear about it? Could you tell at a glance if it was full or over?

- Q-S04. Walk me through the last time you signed up for an event, from deciding to being done. [P4]
  - Probes: What did you type or tap? Was there a moment you were unsure what to do next?

- Q-S05. After you signed up, how did you know it had worked? [P4, P5]
  - Probes: What did you see or hear? What did you do to double-check?

- Q-S06. Right now, how do you find out whether you are registered for a particular event? [P5]
  - Probes: Show me where you look. Does it need a list or table to read?

- Q-S07. Tell me about a time you could not sign up, or ended up signed up twice or for two events at the same time. What happened? [P7, P2]
  - Probes: Did you get told why? Who fixed it?

- Q-S08. Tell me about a time you made a mistake while signing up (wrong event or wrong details). How did you notice, and how did you fix it? [P7]
  - Probes: Could you undo it yourself? How long did it take?

- Q-S09. If you have been on an event committee: how did you learn what you were responsible for and by when, and how did you tell the coordinator it was done? [P6, P4]
  - Probes: Who gave you the task, and how? What happened when you missed a date? (Skip if the participant has not been an organizer.)

- Q-S10. Which words have you seen for being signed up (registered, confirmed, enrolled, on the waiting list, something else)? Do they mean the same thing to you? [P10]
  - Probes: Which word is clearest to you? Which one confused you?

- Q-S11. Tell me about a time a college website, app or notice was hard for you to use. [P8]
  - Probes: What made it hard (small text, bright sunlight, slow internet, no data, using only a phone, comfort with technology)? What did you do instead?

### Task observation (optional)

- Q-S12. Please show me how you find an event and register for it today, saying aloud what you are doing. [P3, P4]
  - Probes: Note the steps, hesitations and mistakes. Do not help unless asked.

### Wrap-up

- Q-S13. Is there anything about finding or joining events that we did not ask about but should have? [P8]

Thank the participant, explain what happens with the notes, and say that results will be shared if they wish.

## Section C: Admins

### Intro script (read aloud)

"Thank you for your time. We are students studying how college events are approved and monitored, and we are designing a better system. This is voluntary. There are no right or wrong answers; we want to hear how things really work for you today. You can skip any question or stop at any time. We would like to take notes, and we will only record audio if you say yes. We will not record your face and we will not write your name; your notes are filed under a session id only."

### Warm-up

- Q-A01. Tell me about your role and how you are involved when a college event is planned. [P8]

  - Probes: How long have you done this? Who else is involved in decisions?

- Q-A02. How do requests for approval reach you at the moment (paper, in person, chat, something else)? [P3]

  - Probes: How many do you see in a typical term? Which way do you prefer?

### Core questions

- Q-A03. Walk me through the last event you approved, from the first time you heard about it. [P3, P4]
  - Probes: What did you receive? What did you do next? How did you tell the coordinator?

- Q-A04. Before you said yes, what did you check, and what do you wish had been in front of you? [P2, P3]
  - Probes: Did you check the venue or the dates? How did you know the information was up to date?

- Q-A05. The last time two events wanted the same venue or time, how did you find out and how did you decide? [P2, P6]
  - Probes: How long did it take to notice? Who had to be told?

- Q-A06. How do you find out that an event is in trouble (few registrations, late tasks, a clash)? [P6]
  - Probes: Do you look, or does someone tell you? Is there too much to look at, or too little?

- Q-A07. Which reports, lists or charts do you look at to see venue use or how events are going? What do they tell you, and what do they fail to tell you? [P9]
  - Probes: How long does it take to find one fact? Which one do you trust most?

- Q-A08. Tell me about a time you realised an approval was wrong (wrong date, or it clashed with something). How did you notice and how was it put right? [P7]
  - Probes: Could you change the decision easily? Who had to be told again?

- Q-A09. After you approve or reject an event, how does the coordinator learn about it, and how do you know they understood? [P4]
  - Probes: What words are used? Has there been a misunderstanding?

- Q-A10. Which words do you use for an event you have agreed (approved, confirmed, cleared, something else)? Do coordinators and students use the same words? [P10]
  - Probes: Which word is clearest? Has a different word caused confusion?

- Q-A11. Tell me about a time you or a colleague found a computer, phone or website hard to use for this work. [P8, P7]
  - Probes: What made it hard (comfort with technology, eyesight, small screen, bright light, slow internet)? What did you do instead?

### Task observation (optional)

- Q-A12. Please show me how you approve an event today, saying aloud what you are doing. [P3, P4]
  - Probes: Note the information used, the steps and any hesitation. Do not help unless asked.

### Wrap-up

- Q-A13. Is there anything about approving or monitoring events that we did not ask about but should have? [P8]

Thank the participant, explain what happens with the notes, and say that results will be shared if they wish.
