# Questionnaire

> AI-drafted instrument. This file contains no responses, quotes or findings. Only the team may record results, and only real sessions may be marked evidence.

## Introduction and consent

Read or show this to every respondent before they start.

"This short questionnaire is part of a student project about how college events are planned and managed. Taking part is voluntary. We do not ask for your name, and your answers are anonymous. You can skip any item you do not want to answer, and you can stop at any time. There are no right or wrong answers."

Target completion time is 5 to 8 minutes (drafted, not evidence; the team confirms). Use your own paper copy or a free online form and copy the items from this file; no form app is built (`adr-004-scope`). Give each respondent only the section for their role. Every item ends with a tag such as [P3] linking it to one of the ten HMI problems; the key is in `question-trace.md`. Remove the tags from the copy that respondents see if you prefer.

Scale used in the agreement items: Strongly disagree, Disagree, Neither agree nor disagree, Agree, Strongly agree.

## Section A: Coordinators

- QN-C01. How comfortable are you with using computers and phones for college work? (optional) [P8]
  - Options: Very uncomfortable / Uncomfortable / Neither / Comfortable / Very comfortable / Prefer not to say

- QN-C02. Do you use anything that helps you read or use screens? Choose all that apply. (optional) [P8]
  - Options: Larger text / Screen reader / High contrast / Voice control / Something else / None of these / Prefer not to say

- QN-C03. In the last term, how many events did you create or send for approval? [P1]
  - Options: None / 1 / 2 to 3 / 4 or more

- QN-C04. Creating my last event took more steps and typing than it should have. [P1]
  - Options: Strongly disagree / Disagree / Neither agree nor disagree / Agree / Strongly agree

- QN-C05. In the last term, how often did a venue or time slot clash or get double-booked for an event you were involved in? [P2]
  - Options: Never / Once / 2 to 3 times / 4 or more times / Not sure

- QN-C06. After I last sent an event for approval, it was clear to me what had happened and what came next. [P4]
  - Options: Strongly disagree / Disagree / Neither agree nor disagree / Agree / Strongly agree

- QN-C07. I can tell at a glance which of my events are being prepared, which are waiting for a decision and which are confirmed. [P3]
  - Options: Strongly disagree / Disagree / Neither agree nor disagree / Agree / Strongly agree

- QN-C08. The last time I made a mistake in an event plan, it was easy to find and correct. [P7]
  - Options: Strongly disagree / Disagree / Neither agree nor disagree / Agree / Strongly agree

- QN-C09. I find out quickly when few students have registered or when a student's task is late. [P6]
  - Options: Strongly disagree / Disagree / Neither agree nor disagree / Agree / Strongly agree

- QN-C10. What do you look at to judge how an event is going? Choose all that apply. [P9]
  - Options: Lists of names / Counts and numbers / Charts / Messages from people / Nothing in particular

- QN-C11. In your own words, what is the hardest part of planning an event? [P1]
  - Options: Free text
